By installing or using an App you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell your personal information.
  • Most App features run entirely on your device. Content such as audio from your microphone, photos, files and Bluetooth device lists is processed locally and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • We use a small number of trusted service providers, such as Google Firebase, to understand app usage and fix crashes. They receive limited technical data that does not identify you by name.
  • Payments for paid Apps and in-app items are handled by Amazon. We never see or store your payment card details.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

We only receive information you choose to send us. For example, if you email us for support or fill in a feedback form, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use an App, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, app version, language, screen size, and time zone.
  • Usage information: screens viewed, features used, session length, and similar app-interaction events.
  • Diagnostic and crash data: crash logs, error reports, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and performance data.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or installation identifiers generated by our analytics and crash-reporting providers. In Apps that show ads, this may also include an advertising identifier (see Advertising).
  • Approximate network information: such as IP address, which service providers may use to estimate country or region.

2.3 Information stored only on your device

Many App features save your settings and data on your device only. Examples include preferences, theme, language, saved device names, connection history, and favourites. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Apps and their features.
  • To diagnose crashes and bugs and to improve stability and performance.
  • To understand how the Apps are used, in aggregate, so we can improve them.
  • To deliver purchased content and features, and to verify entitlements via Amazon.
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback.
  • To protect the Apps, our users and us, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Third-party services

We share limited information with the following service providers. They process it on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: app usage statistics. Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy information
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error reporting.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config: remote app settings and feature configuration.
  • Amazon Appstore and Amazon In-App Purchasing: app distribution, payments and purchase verification. Amazon Privacy Notice

We may also disclose information:

  • if required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid government request;
  • to protect the rights, property or safety of Trisol Technologies, our users or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to the transferred information.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising in our paid, ad-free Apps.

6. Purchases on Amazon

Paid Apps, in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Amazon. Amazon collects and handles your payment information under its own terms and privacy notice. From Amazon we receive only the information needed to confirm and deliver your purchase, such as a purchase receipt or entitlement status. We never receive your card number or billing address. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Amazon.

7. Advertising

Paid Apps, such as our ad-free editions, do not display advertising and do not use an advertising identifier.

If a free App shows ads, it will say so on its Amazon Appstore listing. Ad providers such as the Amazon Publisher Services or Google AdMob SDKs may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads at any time in your device settings (on Fire devices: Settings > Privacy > Advertising ID).

8. Data retention

  • Data stored on your device remains until you delete it, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, and no longer than 24 months.

9. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect information, including encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections to our service providers and minimal data collection. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

These include rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA). To exercise any of them, email trisoltechnologies786@gmail.com and name the App you use. Analytics data is linked to an app-instance identifier rather than to your name, so we may ask for details that help us find it. You can also remove locally stored data at any time by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it. If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us. We will delete it promptly.
